# Free shipping

Policy SH-02, version 3. Owner: E-commerce Operations.

## Rules

- Standard shipping is free on orders of **USD 75 or more** after discounts and before tax.
- Loyalty members at the Gold tier get free standard shipping on every order and free expedited shipping on orders of
  USD 150 or more.
- Free shipping does not apply to freight delivery of large items (SH-05).
- If a return brings an order below the threshold, Harbor Goods does not charge the shipping cost back.
