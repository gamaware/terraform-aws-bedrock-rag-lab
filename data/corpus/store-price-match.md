# Price match

Policy ST-01, version 3. Owner: Store Operations.

## Rules

- Harbor Goods matches its own lower price if the price drops within **14 days** of purchase. The customer receives
  the difference as a refund to the original payment method.
- Harbor Goods matches the advertised price of an identical item from a local brick-and-mortar store that has it in
  stock. The item must be the same brand, model and color.
- Online marketplaces, auction sites, clearance and bundle offers are not price matched.
- Staff verify the competitor's price through the ad or the store's own website before matching.
- Limit of one price match per item per customer.
