# Shipping options and delivery times

Policy SH-01, version 5. Owner: E-commerce Operations.

## Options

| Option | Delivery time | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 5 to 7 business days | USD 5.99 |
| Expedited | 2 to 3 business days | USD 12.99 |
| Next day | 1 business day, order by 1 p.m. local time | USD 24.99 |

## Notes

- Delivery times start when the order ships, not when it is placed. Orders ship within 1 business day.
- Next-day delivery is not available for furniture, rugs or items over 70 pounds.
- Orders to PO boxes ship by standard delivery only.
