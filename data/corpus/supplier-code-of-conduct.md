# Supplier code of conduct

Policy SP-05, version 2. Owner: Procurement, Responsible Sourcing.

## Requirements

- No forced, prison or child labor. Workers are at least 16 years old, or older where local law requires.
- Workers are paid at least the legal minimum wage and overtime is voluntary.
- Factories allow unannounced audits by Harbor Goods or an audit firm it appoints.
- Suppliers disclose all subcontractors and factories used for Harbor Goods orders.

## Breaches

A serious breach stops new purchase orders until a corrective action plan is approved. Repeated breaches end the
relationship.
