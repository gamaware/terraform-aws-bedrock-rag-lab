# Returning online orders in a store

Policy RT-05, version 3. Owner: E-commerce Operations.

## Rules

- Items bought on harborgoods.example.com can be returned in any Harbor Goods store, free of charge.
- Staff scan the order barcode from the confirmation message or look up the order number (format HG followed by eight
  digits).
- Refunds go back to the original online payment method and take **3 to 5 business days** to appear.
- Items sold only online (marked "online exclusive") can also be returned in store; staff ship them back to the
  e-commerce warehouse with a return-to-DC label.
- Marketplace items sold by third-party sellers cannot be returned in store. The customer starts the return from the
  seller's order page.

## Mail returns

Customers who prefer mail returns print a prepaid label from their account. A return shipping fee of USD 6.95 is
deducted from the refund unless the item arrived damaged or wrong.
