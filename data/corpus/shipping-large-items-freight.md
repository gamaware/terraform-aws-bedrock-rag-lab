# Large items and freight delivery

Policy SH-05, version 3. Owner: Logistics.

## Rules

- Furniture, rugs over 8 feet and appliances ship by freight carrier with a scheduled **two-hour delivery window**.
- Threshold delivery costs USD 49. Room-of-choice delivery with packaging removal costs USD 99.
- The customer or an adult representative must be present to sign.
- If nobody is present, the second delivery attempt costs USD 49.
- The customer inspects the item before signing. Damage noted on the delivery receipt triggers a free replacement.
