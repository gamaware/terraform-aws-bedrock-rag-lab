# International shipping

Policy SH-04, version 2. Owner: E-commerce Operations.

## Rules

- Harbor Goods ships to the United States, Canada and Mexico only.
- Canada and Mexico orders ship with duties and taxes calculated at checkout; the customer pays no fees on delivery.
- International orders cannot contain furniture, lithium batteries shipped on their own, or aerosols.
- International returns are accepted by mail only, with a prepaid label; refunds exclude the original shipping
  charge.
