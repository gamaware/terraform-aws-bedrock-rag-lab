# Final sale items

Policy RT-04, version 2. Owner: Store Support.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Clearance items marked with a red "Final sale" tag.
- Gift cards and prepaid cards.
- Personalized or engraved products.
- Opened consumables such as candles, cosmetics and food.
- Custom-cut fabric and rugs cut to size.

## Exceptions

A final sale item that is defective can still be exchanged for the same item under the warranty process (WR-03).
Final sale status must be printed on the receipt; if it is missing, treat the item as a regular return under RT-01.
