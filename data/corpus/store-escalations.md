# Customer escalations

Policy ST-05, version 2. Owner: Store Operations.

## Levels

1. Associate: resolves within policy.
2. Shift lead: may approve goodwill up to **USD 50** per case.
3. Store manager: may approve goodwill up to **USD 200** per case and policy exceptions such as GW-3.
4. Customer Care escalation team: anything above USD 200, threats of legal action, media inquiries.

## Rules

- Staff never discuss legal liability with a customer. Any mention of a lawsuit or a lawyer goes straight to level 4.
- Media inquiries are referred to the communications team without comment.
