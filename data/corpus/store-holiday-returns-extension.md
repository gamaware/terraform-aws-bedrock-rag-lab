# Holiday return extension

Policy ST-06, version 2. Owner: Store Operations.

## Rules

- Items bought from the first day of November through the last day of December can be returned until the
  **last day of January** of the following year.
- The extension does not apply to opened electronics (RT-03 keeps its 15-day window) or to final sale items (RT-04).
- Gift receipts issued during the holiday season allow an exchange or store credit at the price paid.
