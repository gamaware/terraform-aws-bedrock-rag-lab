# Supplier onboarding

Policy SP-01, version 3. Owner: Procurement.

## Requirements

- New suppliers complete the vendor form, a tax form, a certificate of insurance with at least USD 2 million general
  liability, and bank details on company letterhead.
- Procurement verifies bank details by calling a number found independently, never the number on the submitted form.
- Suppliers of electrical products provide safety certification for each product line.
- Every supplier signs the supplier code of conduct (SP-05).

## Timing

Onboarding takes about **15 business days** after all documents are received. No purchase order may be issued before
the supplier is active in the vendor master.
