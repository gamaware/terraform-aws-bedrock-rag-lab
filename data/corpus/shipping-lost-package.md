# Lost or late packages

Policy SH-03, version 3. Owner: E-commerce Operations.

## Rules

- A package is considered lost when tracking shows no movement for **5 business days**, or when it is marked
  delivered but the customer did not receive it.
- For a package marked delivered, ask the customer to check with neighbors and the building office, then wait
  **2 days** before opening a claim.
- After a lost-package claim is opened, Harbor Goods sends a replacement or refunds the order, whichever the customer
  prefers. Staff do not wait for the carrier's investigation.
- A customer can receive at most two lost-package replacements in 12 months without review by the loss prevention
  team.

## Carrier claims

Staff open the carrier claim in the shipping portal with the tracking number. Recovered money is credited to the
e-commerce cost center.
