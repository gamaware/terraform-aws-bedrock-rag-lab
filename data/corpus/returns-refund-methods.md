# Refund methods and timing

Policy RT-07, version 3. Owner: Finance, Store Accounting.

## Rules

- Refunds always go to the original payment method when it is available.
- Card refunds take up to **10 business days**, depending on the card issuer.
- Cash purchases are refunded in cash up to USD 250. Above that amount the refund is issued as a check mailed within
  10 business days.
- Purchases paid with a gift card are refunded to a new gift card.
- Split-tender purchases are refunded in the same proportions as the original payment.
- Buy-now-pay-later purchases are refunded to the financing provider, which updates the customer's installment plan.

## Staff notes

Never refund to a different card than the one used for the purchase, even if the customer asks.
