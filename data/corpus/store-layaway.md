# Layaway

Policy ST-07, version 2. Owner: Store Operations.

## Rules

- Layaway is available in store for purchases of USD 100 or more.
- The customer pays a **20 percent deposit** and a USD 5 service fee.
- The balance is paid in installments within **60 days**.
- If the layaway is canceled, the deposit is refunded minus the service fee.
- If the balance is not paid within 60 days, the items return to stock and the payments, minus the fee, are refunded
  as store credit.
