# Damaged inbound shipments

Policy SP-03, version 2. Owner: Logistics, Receiving.

## Rules

- Receiving inspects every pallet on arrival and notes visible damage on the carrier's delivery receipt before
  signing.
- Damaged cartons are photographed and set aside in the quarantine zone.
- Receiving files a discrepancy report within **48 hours**. Suppliers credit damaged or short-shipped goods on the
  next invoice.
- Shipments that arrive more than 25 percent damaged may be refused in full.
