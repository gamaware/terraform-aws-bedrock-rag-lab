# Gifts and hospitality from suppliers

Policy SP-06, version 2. Owner: Compliance.

## Rules

- Employees may accept gifts from suppliers worth up to **USD 50** per supplier per year.
- Cash, gift cards and loans are never accepted, whatever the amount.
- Business meals are allowed when the supplier attends and the meal is reasonable. Travel paid by a supplier needs
  the approval of a vice president.
- Gifts above the limit are declined politely or handed to Compliance, which donates them.
- All gifts above USD 25 are recorded in the gifts register.
