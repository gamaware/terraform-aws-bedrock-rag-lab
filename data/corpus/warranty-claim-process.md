# Warranty claim process

Policy WR-03, version 3. Owner: Customer Care.

## Steps

1. Confirm the purchase through the receipt or the loyalty purchase history.
2. Confirm the item is inside the warranty period (WR-01).
3. Open a warranty case in the support tool with the product code, the defect description and photos.
4. Customer Care answers within **2 business days** with a decision: repair, replacement or refund.
5. For approved replacements, the store may swap the item at the counter if it is in stock.

## Proof of purchase

Without any proof of purchase, a warranty claim cannot be approved. Staff may offer the manufacturer's contact
details instead.
