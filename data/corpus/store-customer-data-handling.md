# Handling customer personal data

Policy ST-04, version 3. Owner: Privacy Office.

## Rules

- Collect only the data a task needs. A return needs the order or loyalty number, not the customer's full address.
- Never write customer card numbers, full ID numbers or passwords in notes, emails or chat tools.
- Customer data may not be pasted into tools that are not approved by the Privacy Office.
- A customer who asks for a copy or deletion of their data is directed to the privacy request form; staff do not
  process these requests in store.
- A suspected data incident is reported to the Privacy Office within **1 hour** of discovery.
