# Items damaged on arrival

Policy RT-06, version 2. Owner: E-commerce Operations.

## Rules

- A customer who receives a damaged or wrong item must report it within **7 days** of delivery.
- The customer sends photos of the item and of the outer box through the order page.
- Harbor Goods ships a replacement at no cost, or refunds in full including the original shipping charge.
- For damaged items under USD 50, staff do not ask for the item back; the customer may dispose of it.
- For furniture and large items, the carrier inspects the item before a replacement is sent (see SH-05).

## Staff notes

Tag the case "DOA" in the support tool so the carrier claim (SH-03) can recover the cost.
