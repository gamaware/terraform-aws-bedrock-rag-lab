# Opened electronics returns

Policy RT-03, version 3. Owner: Merchandising, Electronics.

## Rules

- Opened electronics (laptops, tablets, headphones, smart speakers, cameras) may be returned within **15 days**.
- A **restocking fee of 15 percent** applies to opened laptops, tablets and cameras. It is waived if the item is
  defective or if the customer exchanges it for the same model.
- Headphones and earbuds can only be returned opened if they are defective, for hygiene reasons.
- The customer must remove any account lock (for example a device activation lock) before the return. Staff cannot
  accept a locked device.

## Data wipe

Staff run the factory reset checklist EL-7 on every returned device with storage, then record the reset in the return
note before the item goes back to stock or to the vendor.
