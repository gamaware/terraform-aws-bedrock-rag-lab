# Standard return window

Policy RT-01, version 4. Owner: Store Support.

## Scope

Applies to all merchandise bought in a Harbor Goods store or on harborgoods.example.com, except items listed in the
final sale policy (RT-04).

## Rules

- Customers may return most items within **30 days** of purchase for a full refund to the original payment method.
- The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and include all accessories and manuals.
- Loyalty members at the Gold tier get **45 days** instead of 30.
- Mattresses have a 100-night comfort trial instead of the 30-day window; see the furniture section of the warranty
  policy (WR-05) for pickup rules.
- The return window starts on the delivery date for online orders and on the receipt date for store purchases.

## Staff notes

Check the receipt date in the point-of-sale system before accepting a return. If the window has passed by 3 days or
fewer, a store manager may approve store credit as a goodwill exception. Record the exception code GW-3 in the return
note.
