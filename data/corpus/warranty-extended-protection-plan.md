# Extended protection plans

Policy WR-02, version 4. Owner: Customer Care.

## Plans

- Harbor Care plans extend coverage to **2 or 3 years** from the purchase date and add accidental damage for
  electronics.
- A plan can be bought at checkout or within **30 days** of the product purchase.
- Plans are priced by product price band; the current price list is in the point-of-sale system.
- A plan is cancelable within 30 days for a full refund. After that, the refund is prorated by the remaining months.

## Claims

Plan claims are handled by the plan administrator, not by the store. Staff give the customer the plan number and the
claim line printed on the plan certificate.
