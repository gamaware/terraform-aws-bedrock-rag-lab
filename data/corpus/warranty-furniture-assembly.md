# Furniture assembly and mattress trial

Policy WR-05, version 2. Owner: Merchandising, Home.

## Assembly

- Harbor Goods offers in-home assembly for a flat fee of USD 79 per order.
- If the assembly team damages an item or a wall, Harbor Goods pays for the repair. The customer reports it within
  **48 hours** of the visit.
- Self-assembly mistakes, such as a stripped screw hole, are not covered by the warranty.

## Mattress comfort trial

- Mattresses have a **100-night comfort trial**. Customers must keep the mattress for at least 30 nights before
  starting a trial return.
- One trial return per household. Harbor Goods picks up the mattress at no charge and donates it.
