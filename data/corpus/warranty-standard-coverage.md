# Standard product warranty

Policy WR-01, version 3. Owner: Customer Care.

## Coverage

- Harbor Goods private-label products (brands Harbor Home, Harbor Tech and Keel) carry a **1-year limited warranty**
  against manufacturing defects from the purchase date.
- Harbor Home furniture frames carry a **5-year** frame warranty.
- Third-party brands carry the manufacturer's warranty. Staff direct the customer to the manufacturer after the
  30-day return window has passed.

## What the warranty provides

Repair, replacement with the same or a comparable item, or a refund when neither repair nor replacement is possible.
The choice is made by Customer Care, not by the store.
