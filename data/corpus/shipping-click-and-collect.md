# Click and collect

Policy SH-07, version 3. Owner: Store Operations.

## Rules

- Customers can buy online and pick up in store for free. Orders are ready within **2 hours** during store opening
  hours when the item is in stock locally.
- Orders are held for **7 days**. After that they are canceled and refunded automatically.
- The customer shows the pickup code and a photo ID. Someone else can pick up the order if the customer adds their
  name to the order before pickup.
- Curbside pickup is available at stores with marked parking bays.
