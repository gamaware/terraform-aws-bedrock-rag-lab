# Warranty exclusions

Policy WR-04, version 2. Owner: Customer Care.

## Not covered

- Normal wear and tear, scratches and fading.
- Damage from misuse, accidents, or repairs by someone other than an authorized service center.
- Products used commercially, for example a Harbor Home sofa used in a hotel lobby.
- Consumable parts such as batteries after 6 months, filters and bulbs.
- Items bought from unauthorized resellers.

## Water damage

Water damage to electronics is excluded from the standard warranty. It is covered only by a Harbor Care plan with
accidental damage (WR-02).
