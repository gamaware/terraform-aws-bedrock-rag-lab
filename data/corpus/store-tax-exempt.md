# Tax-exempt purchases

Policy ST-08, version 2. Owner: Finance, Tax.

## Rules

- Nonprofits, schools and government agencies can buy tax-free with a valid exemption certificate on file.
- The certificate is registered once through the business account portal; staff do not accept paper certificates at
  the register.
- The purchase must be paid with the organization's payment method, not a personal card.
- Tax-exempt purchases cannot be made for personal use. Misuse ends the exemption on the account.
