# Supplier invoices and payment terms

Policy SP-02, version 4. Owner: Finance, Accounts Payable.

## Terms

- Standard payment terms are **net 60 days** from the invoice receipt date.
- Suppliers may choose early payment at net 10 with a **2 percent discount**.
- Invoices must quote the purchase order number. Invoices without a purchase order number are returned unpaid.
- Harbor Goods pays only against a three-way match: purchase order, goods receipt and invoice. Differences above
  2 percent go to the buyer for review.

## Changes to bank details

A change to supplier bank details is verified by a call back to the supplier contact on file and approved by two
people in Accounts Payable.
