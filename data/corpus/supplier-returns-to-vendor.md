# Returns to vendor

Policy SP-04, version 3. Owner: Merchandising.

## Rules

- Defective customer returns of third-party brands are sent back to the vendor monthly, consolidated by vendor.
- A return-to-vendor authorization number is required from the vendor before shipping.
- Vendors credit the cost price plus freight within 30 days of receiving the return.
- Items the vendor will not take back are liquidated through the outlet channel or recycled; they are never sold as
  new.
