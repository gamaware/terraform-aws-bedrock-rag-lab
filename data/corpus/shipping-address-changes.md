# Changing a delivery address

Policy SH-06, version 2. Owner: E-commerce Operations.

## Rules

- Customers can change the delivery address from their account until the order status becomes "Picking".
- After that, staff may request a carrier redirect once the package ships. Redirects cost USD 10 and are only
  possible within the same country.
- For fraud prevention, an address change to a different state on an order above USD 500 requires a call back to the
  phone number on the account.
