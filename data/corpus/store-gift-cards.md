# Gift cards

Policy ST-02, version 2. Owner: Finance, Store Accounting.

## Rules

- Gift cards never expire and have no fees.
- Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash except where state law requires it for balances under USD 10.
- A lost gift card can be replaced only with the original purchase receipt, for the remaining balance at the time the
  card is reported lost.
- Gift cards cannot be used to buy other gift cards.
- Staff never read out a full card number or security code over the phone.
