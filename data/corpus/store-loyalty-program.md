# Harbor Rewards loyalty program

Policy ST-03, version 4. Owner: Marketing.

## Tiers

| Tier | Yearly spend | Benefits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member | Any | 1 point per dollar, birthday coupon |
| Silver | USD 500 | 1.5 points per dollar |
| Gold | USD 1,500 | 2 points per dollar, 45-day returns, free standard shipping |

## Points

- 100 points are worth USD 5 in rewards, issued monthly.
- Rewards expire **90 days** after they are issued.
- Points are removed when the purchase that earned them is returned.
- A customer can merge two accounts once, through Customer Care.
